The cost of an interior designer in Israel depends less on the designer's name than on what you ask them to do. Most designers charge in one of five ways: per square metre, a fixed project fee, by the hour, a percentage of the works, or a one-off consultation. The price then moves with the size of the home, the depth of the renovation, how much custom joinery is drawn, and whether the designer stays on site until the last handle is fitted.

This guide explains each fee model, what pushes a quote up or down, and the costs a good designer takes off your renovation budget: everything you need to answer "כמה עולה מעצבת פנים" before the first call.

## How interior designers in Israel charge

### Per square metre

The most common model for apartments and houses in Israel. The designer quotes a price per m² of the area being designed, multiplied by the size of the home. It is easy to compare between studios, and it scales sensibly: a 130 m² family apartment involves more rooms, more drawings and more decisions than a 50 m² city flat.

The catch is that m² says nothing about depth. A full renovation of a 60 m² apartment with custom millwork on every wall can demand more design hours than a light refresh of a 150 m² house. Check what the per-m² price includes.

### Fixed project fee

A single agreed price for a defined scope, usually paid in stages: on signing, on approval of the concept, on delivery of the working drawings, and during supervision. This is common for well-defined projects, commercial spaces and anything where the client wants certainty.

A fixed fee is only as good as the scope written next to it. A clear proposal lists the rooms, the drawings, the number of design revisions and the number of site visits.

### Hourly

Used for consultations, small jobs, and work outside an agreed scope (an extra round of changes, a room added halfway). It is fair for open-ended work, and frustrating for a whole renovation, because nobody can see the final number.

### Percentage of the works

More common abroad and in large commercial or hospitality projects than in Israeli apartments. The fee is a share of the construction and furnishing budget. It ties the designer's work to the size of the project, but some clients feel it rewards spending, so it is usually paired with a budget the designer must design to.

### One-off consultation

A single paid meeting at your home: a walk-through, layout advice, colours, materials, or a second opinion on a contractor's plan. It suits people who will manage the renovation themselves and want a professional eye at the decisive moment. You leave with ideas and direction, not drawings a contractor can build from.

### A general picture of the market

Designers in Israel are free to set their own prices, and quotes for the same apartment can differ several times over. A low figure per m² can hide a thin scope, and a higher one can include drawings, site visits and sourcing that you would otherwise pay for separately, so set the number against what it buys. Always check whether a quote includes VAT, since many Israeli professionals quote before VAT.

## What actually moves the cost of an interior designer in Israel

The same apartment can produce very different quotes. These are the factors that explain most of the gap.

| Factor | Why it changes the fee |
|---|---|
| Scope | Furnishing and styling only, a kitchen and bathrooms, or a full renovation with a new layout are different amounts of work. |
| Size and number of rooms | More rooms mean more plans, elevations and decisions; wet rooms and kitchens are the most drawing-intensive. |
| Custom joinery | Every built-in wardrobe, kitchen, TV wall or slatted partition needs its own detailed drawings and coordination with the carpenter. |
| Supervision | Visiting site, checking the contractor's work against the drawings and answering questions during works takes time across months. |
| Sourcing and procurement | Selecting, ordering and following up on furniture, lighting and finishes is a service of its own. |
| Location and distance | A home far from the studio, or abroad, means travel or a remote way of working. |
| Timeline | A compressed schedule demands more of the studio's time at once. |

### Scope: from styling to full renovation

A layout and furniture selection for a new apartment from a developer is a modest job. A full renovation of an older apartment, with demolition, plumbing, electrics, air conditioning, lighting, kitchen, bathrooms and joinery, is the largest. Most projects sit between.

### Custom joinery

Custom carpentry is where a designer's drawings carry the most weight, and where fees reflect it. In [XL APT](/work/xl-apt), a 60 m² apartment in a Tel Aviv tower, custom millwork covers all the walls, giving texture to every surface and hiding the storage behind it. That kind of result is built on many sheets of precise drawings, each coordinated with the carpenter.

### Supervision

In Israel this is usually called design supervision (פיקוח עליון): the designer visits the site at key stages to make sure what is built matches what was drawn. It is different from project management, where someone runs the contractor, schedule and payments day to day. Ask which of the two is included, how many visits are planned, and what an extra visit costs.

### Sourcing

Some designers hand over a list of products and leave the purchasing to you; others order, track deliveries and check what arrives. The second takes far more time, and it is also where a lot of renovation stress disappears.

## What you are paying for: the deliverables

When comparing quotes, compare what you receive, not just the number. A full design service in Israel typically includes some or all of the following:

- Measurement of the existing home and a survey drawing
- Layout options and a chosen floor plan
- A concept: mood, materials, colour and furniture direction
- 3D visualisations of key spaces (not always, and sometimes charged separately)
- Working drawings for the contractor: demolition and construction, electrical and lighting, plumbing, air conditioning, flooring and tiling, ceilings
- Detailed joinery drawings for kitchen, wardrobes and built-ins
- A specification of finishes, sanitaryware and fittings
- Help comparing contractor quotes
- Site visits during works
- Furniture and lighting selection, and sometimes ordering

Ask each designer which of these items their proposal covers, and which are charged separately.

## Where an interior designer saves you money

A designer's fee is easy to see. The money they save is spread across the project, so it is easier to miss.

### Mistakes that are expensive to undo

The costliest renovation errors are the ones discovered after the tiles are down: a socket behind a wardrobe, a kitchen drain in the wrong place, an air-conditioning duct that forces a lowered ceiling across the living room, a shower without enough fall. Every one of them is cheap on paper and expensive on site. Detailed drawings catch them while they are still lines.

### Accurate, comparable contractor quotes

When three contractors price the same set of drawings and the same bill of quantities, you can compare their quotes properly. When each prices their own interpretation of a conversation, you cannot, and the differences return later as "extras".

### Coordination between trades

A renovation in Israel brings together a contractor, an electrician, a plumber, an air-conditioning installer, a carpenter, a kitchen supplier, a stone fabricator, and sometimes an aluminium supplier for windows. When they work from one set of drawings, the electrician knows where the carpenter's hidden lighting goes, and the kitchen fits the plumbing. Without that, you become the coordinator.

### Buying once

A sofa that does not fit, a rug in the wrong size, lighting at the wrong colour temperature: a plan that fixes dimensions and finishes up front means buying once.

### Using every square metre

In smaller Israeli apartments, the value of good space planning is concrete. In [GY APT](/work/gy-apt), a 130 m² family apartment in Glil Yam, the studio accompanied the family from the real-estate purchase to move-in, maximising storage while keeping a generous open living area and expanding the kitchen. Built-in storage done well can make a separate storage room, or a larger apartment, unnecessary.

## How to compare quotes from interior designers

1. **Give every designer the same brief.** Size, rooms, what is changing, what stays, your timeline and an honest budget range for the works.
2. **Ask for the scope in writing.** Which drawings, how many revision rounds, how many site visits, whether 3D and sourcing are included.
3. **Ask how changes are charged.** A clear answer here prevents most fee disputes.
4. **Look at completed projects, not only renders.** Built work shows whether the drawings held up on site. You can see ours on the [projects page](/projects).
5. **Check the title.** "Interior designer" and "interior architect" are common descriptions; "architect" (אדריכל) is a registered title in Israel. Our guide to [interior architect vs interior designer](/journal/interior-architect-vs-interior-designer) explains who does what.

If you are at an earlier stage and still choosing who to work with, [how to choose an interior designer in Tel Aviv](/journal/interior-designer-tel-aviv) covers what to look for beyond price.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does an interior designer cost in Israel?

There is no single price. Designers in Israel usually charge per square metre, a fixed project fee, hourly, a percentage of the works, or for a one-off consultation. The final figure depends on scope, the size of the home, custom joinery, supervision and sourcing, so the most useful comparison is between written proposals for the same brief.

### Is it cheaper to pay per m² or a fixed fee?

Neither is cheaper by nature. Per m² is easy to compare between studios; a fixed fee gives certainty when the scope is clearly defined. In both cases what matters is the list of drawings, revision rounds and site visits included.

### Does the designer's fee include supervision of the works?

Not always. Design supervision (פיקוח עליון) means site visits to check the work against the drawings, and some designers include a set number of visits while others charge per visit. Project management, running the contractor and schedule day to day, is a separate service.

### Can I hire a designer for a consultation only?

Yes. A one-off consultation suits people who will manage the renovation themselves and want professional direction on layout, materials or colours. It will not give you the working drawings a contractor needs to price and build a full renovation accurately.

### Is an interior designer worth it for a small apartment?

Often more so than for a large one. In a small apartment every centimetre of storage, every door swing and every socket position matters, and mistakes are harder to hide. A well-planned 50 or 60 m² apartment can live like a much larger one.
